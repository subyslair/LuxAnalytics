RFMCalculator revision handoff

Replace src/RFMCalculator.cpp with the supplied RFMCalculator.cpp.
Keep existing Models.h and RFMCalculator.h unchanged.
Do not leave another RFM implementation .cpp in src/; the Makefile compiles all src/*.cpp.
Original Member 3 attribution is retained. Ask Member 3 to review the changes.

Policy:
- Fixed reference date: 2026-11-24; not the machine's current date.
- Invalid dates and transactions after that date are excluded.
- Only positive finite amounts are accepted. Refunds and zero amounts are excluded entirely.
- R is days since latest accepted purchase; F is accepted receipt count;
  M is sum of accepted purchases, NOT net revenue after refunds.
- Customers with no accepted purchases are omitted.
- log1p + min-max normalization only, with inverted Recency score.
- Raw R/F/M preserved. rfm_score is in [0,1], larger is better.
- Invalid weights, including an overflowing weight sum, fall back to equal weights.
- cluster_id and tier/action fields remain for downstream modules.

Test (from project root after copying the test into tests/):
g++ -std=c++17 -Wall -Wextra -pedantic -Iinclude src/RFMCalculator.cpp tests/test_rfm_revision.cpp -o rfm_revision_tests
./rfm_revision_tests

Do not compile the test together with src/main.cpp, and do not use -DNDEBUG.
Tests validate the supplied headers and RFM module only; no KMeans or full pipeline test was performed.
Known limitations: date is hardcoded; finite transaction amounts may still overflow when aggregated;
refund netting is intentionally unsupported; year 0000 is not explicitly rejected by the parser.
